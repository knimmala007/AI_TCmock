How it works

From first question to closed package, in five steps

Every screen below is from the running app, using the real 118 Cottage Grove Ave package.

1Dashboard

See every deal at a glance

Start from one dashboard with each property, how far along it is, and what needs doing this week.

  • Progress per property, from listing to escrow
  • Upcoming tasks with due dates
  • A live feed of uploads, signatures and messages
2Agent

One conversation with the AI fills every form

The agent asks one question at a time. Each answer fills every form that needs it, and the agent shows its reasoning and confidence so you can verify or override.

  • Questions sorted so the ones that unblock the most forms come first
  • Forms added or removed by state, county and city rules
  • Every unfinished form names the question holding it up
3Broker

The broker reviews and approves

A guided workflow takes the broker through flagged fields, AI selections and approvals, then sends each document to the right party.

  • Full document set with fill status
  • Unresolved fields flagged, never guessed
  • Progress toward the two-hour target
4Seller

Sellers see their disclosure package

Sellers follow their deal live and see only what they need to act on.

  • Each disclosure with its status and fill level
  • The exact missing item, such as permit history
  • The buyer's appraisal and lender file stay locked
5Buyer

Buyers track their side to closing

Buyers see the property, every milestone from pre-approval to close, and the documents that are theirs.

  • Milestones from pre-approval to closed
  • Title, deposit and appraisal in one place
  • Seller prep forms and the listing agreement stay private
